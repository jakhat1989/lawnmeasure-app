Find an address, then select Draw lawn to begin.

Kentucky 3-inch imagery, government regional aerials, and nationwide federal imagery. Search a property to see which source loads.

No ArcGIS World Imagery subscription, Mapbox token or account is required for these government image sources. Kentucky uses KyFromAbove. In parts of Illinois, the app requests Cook County 2025 or Illinois 2025 six-inch photography; in parts of Florida, it requests county or water-management aerials. All other locations use federal USDA/USGS imagery, which ranges in resolution and may be blurry. Some government imagery servers are hosted on ArcGIS REST software, but the app does not use Esri World Imagery or require an Esri API key. Three-inch aerial coverage cannot be guaranteed nationwide.

Photograph date, coverage, and commercial-reuse terms differ among providers. Confirm site conditions before issuing final quotes. Drawing area is approximate, not a property survey.

Enable hands-free email delivery

When not configured, the Email button opens your phone or computer email app with the customer in To and the business owner in CC. You still need to review and press Send. To send automatically from LawnQuote:

  1. Verify a domain you own at Resend Domains.
  2. Create a sending-only API key at Resend API Keys.
  3. In the LawnQuote Render service, open Environment and add RESEND_API_KEY with the new secret key and RESEND_FROM_EMAIL with a sender such as quotes@yourdomain.com (it must match your verified domain).
  4. Choose Save and deploy. Refresh LawnQuote. The button will switch to automatic delivery.

Keep your API key private; never paste it into a quote, GitHub, or chat. No sending domain is configured yet.
