Enable hands-free email delivery

When not configured, the Email button opens your phone or computer email app with the customer in To and the business owner in CC. You still need to review and press Send. To send automatically from LawnQuote:

  1. Verify a domain you own at Resend Domains.
  2. Create a sending-only API key at Resend API Keys.
  3. In the LawnQuote Render service, open Environment and add RESEND_API_KEY with the new secret key and RESEND_FROM_EMAIL with a sender such as quotes@yourdomain.com (it must match your verified domain).
  4. Choose Save and deploy. Refresh LawnQuote. The button will switch to automatic delivery.

Keep your API key private; never paste it into a quote, GitHub, or chat. No sending domain is configured yet.
