Activate sharper nationwide aerial imagery — ArcGIS

The app supports Esri ArcGIS Location Platform World Imagery. It works across all 50 states. Photo detail varies and some areas will still have lower resolution.

Create an ArcGIS Location Platform account and a browser API key with Location services → Basemaps access. Restrict the key's allowed referrers to https://lawnquote-owner-tool.onrender.com. Check Esri pricing and imagery rights for your business.

In Render → LawnQuote Owner → Environment, add ARCGIS_API_KEY with your ArcGIS browser API key as the value. Save and redeploy, then refresh LawnQuote. Do not put your key into GitHub or this chat; restrict its usage by domain.

After setup, ArcGIS World Imagery becomes the default. Kentucky 3-inch imagery and nationwide USGS imagery remain available as alternatives in the map selector.

Nationwide: ArcGIS World Imagery when configured. Backups: KyFromAbove and USGS. Photograph age, sharpness, and availability vary by location. Drawn areas are estimates, not surveys.

Enable hands-free email delivery

When not configured, the Email button opens your phone or computer email app with the customer in To and the business owner in CC. You still need to review and press Send. To send automatically from LawnQuote:

  1. Verify a domain you own at Resend Domains.
  2. Create a sending-only API key at Resend API Keys.
  3. In the LawnQuote Render service, open Environment and add RESEND_API_KEY with the new secret key and RESEND_FROM_EMAIL with a sender such as quotes@yourdomain.com (it must match your verified domain).
  4. Choose Save and deploy. Refresh LawnQuote. The button will switch to automatic delivery.

Keep your API key private; never paste it into a quote, GitHub, or chat. No sending domain is configured yet.
